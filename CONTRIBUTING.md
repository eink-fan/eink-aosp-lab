# Contributing

Do not submit proprietary device material, copied stock-source fragments,
vendor headers, binaries, waveforms, calibration data, partitions, logs, or
credentials. A pull request must identify the provenance of every added file.

Keep hardware integrations behind `einklab::Engine`. The authored Neo 2
same-build integration belongs under `android/neo2-eink/`; extracted payload
bytes belong only in ignored local storage. Do not add payload manifests or
hashes to issues, commits, test fixtures, or documentation.

By submitting a contribution, you release your copyright interest in that
contribution under [The Unlicense](LICENSE), to the extent you hold those
rights. Do not submit material you lack the right to release.
